ACME Corp Remote Work Policy
Published: January 2026

1. Core Working Hours
All engineering and product teams must be online and available for synchronous collaboration between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM Central European Time (CET). Outside of these core hours, team members can set their own flexible schedules.

2. Equipment and Stipends
The company provides a baseline technology hardware setup including a corporate laptop, an external monitor, and peripheral devices. Employees working remotely are eligible for a one-time internet infrastructure stipend of €150 to upgrade their home network.

3. Annual Leave and Time Off
Full-time corporate employees receive 30 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Vacation requests should be submitted via the HR portal at least two weeks in advance. Unused vacation days can roll over to the next calendar year but must be used before March 31st of that subsequent year.
